FARMER GUIDE

Get a better crop analysis

A good photo produces better visual evidence. Follow these simple steps before scanning.

📷 1. Take a clear photo

DO
Good lighting

Take the photo in daylight where possible.

DO
Focus on the plant

Keep leaves, stems or affected areas visible.

DO
Keep it steady

Avoid blurry images.

AVOID
Random photos

CropPilot will reject images that do not appear to contain a plant or crop.

🌱 2. Select the correct crop

Choose the crop that appears in the image. If the selected crop does not match the image, CropPilot will warn you rather than silently assuming the selection is correct.

🔬 3. Understand the AI result

Confidence

Confidence represents how strongly the model supports its visual assessment. It is not a guarantee of diagnosis.

Severity

Healthy, Watch, Concern or Unknown helps prioritize what deserves attention.

Recommended action

CropPilot focuses on practical next steps instead of unsafe chemical prescriptions.

🛡️ 4. Use AI responsibly

CropPilot is a first-line visual triage tool. A photograph cannot replace laboratory testing, an agricultural expert or local field inspection.

  • Verify important diagnoses.
  • Follow local agricultural guidance.
  • Follow product labels for any approved treatment.
  • Do not apply chemicals based only on an AI result.

🌐 5. Choose your language

CropPilot supports:

🇬🇧 English English advisory
🇮🇳 తెలుగు Telugu advisory
🇮🇳 हिन्दी Hindi advisory
🇮🇳 தமிழ் / ಕನ್ನಡ Tamil and Kannada

🔊 Voice advisory

After an analysis, use the Voice button to hear the advisory in the selected language.
